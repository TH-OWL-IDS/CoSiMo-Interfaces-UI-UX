# 001 — CoSiMo Character Visual Identity

**Date:** 2026-05-28
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

CoSiMo is a mobility concierge that accompanies passengers across multiple touchpoints — app, cabin, gadgets, and station. It needs a visual identity that feels approachable and emotionally readable without being tied to a specific platform, screen size, or technology. The character must work across diverse user groups, including people with disabilities, older adults, and first-time users.

## Options Considered

- **Illustrated mascot** — a character with a fixed body, colour palette, and simple face. Recognisable but heavy, platform-dependent, and risks feeling childish or branded.
- **Abstract icon / logo mark** — geometric shape used as a stand-in. Neutral but emotionally flat; hard to convey states like calm, attentive, or concerned.
- **Minimal floating face** — no face boundary, only essential marks (eyes, nose, mouth). Reduces to the emotional signal itself.

## Decision

CoSiMo's character is expressed as a minimal floating face: black marks on white, no bounding shape, no body. The face consists of three elements only — eyes (dots or dashes), an angular L-shaped nose, and a mouth (curved line or open circle). The hand-drawn quality is intentional: warm and human, not clinical or overly polished.

Four emotional states are defined as the core vocabulary:

| State | Eyes | Mouth |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral | dots | gentle curve |
| Smiling | dot + raised dash | wider curve |
| Content | both closed (arcs) | soft curve |
| Surprised | dots | open circle |

The character has no fixed form or face boundary — it is not tied to a screen, a device, or a surface. It appears wherever CoSiMo communicates.

## Consequences

- The character remains legible at very small sizes and in low-contrast environments.
- Emotional states are communicated without text — relevant for multilingual and accessibility contexts.
- New emotional states can be added by extending the same mark vocabulary without redesigning the character.
- The lack of a body or fixed form means CoSiMo can appear in any interface context without layout constraints.
- Colour, animation, and sound are intentionally left open — this decision fixes only the visual mark language.
