# Example Flow — Two Friends, One with Back Pain

**Scenario:** Kyra and Johannes are out in the evening.
Johannes has back pain and can't cycle home. Kyra is with him.
They have 2 bikes. They're 4 minutes from the nearest MonoCab station.
First-time users.

---

## 1. Discovery
Johannes searches the App Store, finds the CoSiMo app and downloads it.
Kyra opens the website on her phone as a fallback.

## 2. First Contact with CoSiMo
Johannes opens the app. CoSiMo greets him.
He chooses Quick Access — no persona setup right now.

## 3. Group Setup
CoSiMo asks: "Are you travelling alone or with someone?"
Johannes selects: with one other person and adds Kyra to the trip.

Kyra is now part of the journey — she receives the same trip info,
departure timer, and station directions as Johannes.
From this point, CoSiMo treats the trip as shared:
capacity, timing, and check-in are all calculated for two people and two bikes.

## 4. Moment Check
CoSiMo asks: "How are you feeling right now?"
Johannes indicates he is in pain and needs a comfortable ride.
CoSiMo acknowledges and adjusts its suggestions accordingly.

## 5. Booking
- 2 bikes
- No sharing — private cab
- CoSiMo confirms a MonoCab with bike capacity is available
- Nearest MonoCab: 4 min walk, ready in 6 min

## 6. Departure Timer
CoSiMo sets a timer based on Johannes's mobility profile.
Reminder fires: "Time to head to the station — leave in 3 minutes."
Both receive the reminder. Johannes gets directions handed off to Apple Maps.

## 7. Arrival & Check-in
As Johannes and Kyra approach the station, CoSiMo detects proximity
and activates the entrance lighting.

CoSiMo asks: "Are you at the station?"
Johannes confirms via app.

CoSiMo guides them to the terminal — in their direction of movement,
in the extended entrance area. Johannes scans via app for both of them.

CoSiMo: "Hello Johannes and Kyra! Your MonoCab is ready."

The MonoCab screen shows instructions: where to place the bikes,
where to sit, how to strap the bikes in, and to stay clear of the door area.

## 8. Post-Ride
After arrival at destination, CoSiMo asks:
"Would you like me to navigate you home?"
Johannes confirms. CoSiMo asks if trip data may be saved to improve future rides.
Johannes agrees. Kyra skips.
